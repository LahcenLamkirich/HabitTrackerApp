Why Daily Check

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

No sign-ups, no feeds, no clutter. Just your habits for today and the motivation to keep going.

✅

A checklist for today

Open the app and see exactly what's on for today. One tap marks a habit done, and your progress ring fills up.

📅

Schedules that fit your life

Run a habit every day, on weekdays, on weekends, or on the exact days you choose. Days off never break your streak.

🔔

Gentle reminders

Set a reminder time for each habit, plus an optional follow-up nudge if you still haven't checked it off.

🔥

Streaks & badges

Every day you show up keeps the flame alive. Unlock milestone badges from 7 days all the way to a full year.

📊

Insights

See what you've completed, missed and skipped, and which habits are leading your personal leaderboard.

🌙

Made for night owls

Choose when your day resets. If you're up past midnight, that late-night check-in still counts for today.
