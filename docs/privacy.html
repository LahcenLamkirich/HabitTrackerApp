Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

In short: Daily Check does not collect, store, or share any of your personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Who we are

Daily Check is a habit tracker app for Android and iOS. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information we collect

We don't collect any information. Daily Check has no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking tools. We have no servers that receive your data.

Data stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following only on your device:

  • The habits you create (name, icon, notes, schedule and reminder time)
  • Your daily check-in history, which is used to calculate streaks and insights
  • Your app settings, such as reminder preferences and when your day resets

This data never leaves your device unless you export it yourself (see below). If you uninstall the app, this data is deleted.

Backups

You can export your habits and history to a backup file from the app's settings. The file is saved to a location you choose, and you decide what to do with it. You can also restore from a backup file you select. We never receive or have access to these files.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Daily Check schedules reminders locally on your device. No notification data is sent to us or to any third party. You can turn reminders off at any time in the app or in your phone's settings.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show your habit reminders.
  • Run at startup: to restore your scheduled reminders after your phone restarts.
  • Vibration: so reminders can vibrate.

Children's privacy

Daily Check does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date above. If we ever start collecting data, we'll update this policy before we do.
