# Privacy Policy for Daily Check

**Last updated: 25 September 2026**

Daily Check is a habit tracker made by Lahcen Lamkirich. This policy explains
what the app does with your information. The short version: it keeps
everything on your phone and sends nothing anywhere.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Daily Check has no account system, no analytics, no advertising,
and no servers of its own. We cannot see your habits, and we have no way to
identify you.

## What the app stores on your device

Everything you enter stays in the app's private storage on your phone:

- the habits you create, including their names, notes, icons, reminder times
  and weekly schedules
- your check-in history: which habits you completed, skipped or missed, and on
  which dates
- your app settings, such as your reminder preferences and the hour your day
  starts

This data never leaves your device unless you choose to export it (see
**Your backups** below). Uninstalling Daily Check permanently deletes all of
it.

## Permissions the app asks for

- **Notifications** — so reminders for your habits can appear at the times you
  set. Reminders are generated on your device; nothing is sent through a
  server. You can decline this permission and still use the app, or turn
  reminders off at any time in Settings.
- **Run at startup** — so your scheduled reminders survive a restart. Without
  it, reminders would silently stop after you reboot your phone.

## Your backups

Settings contains **Back up my habits**, which writes a JSON file containing
your habits, history and settings, and lets you choose where to save it. Once
you save that file, it is yours and outside the app's control — if you place it
in a cloud service such as Google Drive or iCloud, that service's own privacy
policy applies to it. **Restore from a backup** reads a file you select and
replaces the app's current contents with it.

We never upload, read, or transmit these files.

## Children

Daily Check does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age,
because it does not collect information at all.

## Third-party services

Daily Check includes no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services,
and embeds no third-party trackers.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version adds optional
cloud sync — the updated policy will be published here and the "last updated"
date above will change. Any feature that transmits your data off your device
would be opt-in.
